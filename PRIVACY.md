# Privacy Policy for Cleanup for Amazon

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Cleanup for Amazon does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any
user data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, and no
tracking of any kind.

## What the extension does with data

- Your settings (which page areas are hidden or visible, and any
  schedule windows you define) are stored in your browser's extension
  storage on your own device.
- Settings never leave your browser. The extension makes zero network
  requests.
- The extension does not read, record, or transmit anything from the
  Amazon pages you visit. It only adds or removes CSS classes to hide
  the page sections you chose.

## Permissions

- **storage**: saves your settings locally in the browser.
- **alarms**: wakes the extension at the start and end of your schedule
  windows, and to end a temporary Quick Hide.
- **Host access to Amazon sites**: required to hide page sections on
  Amazon itself. Nothing is read from or sent to these sites.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the change will appear in this file's
history in the public repository.
